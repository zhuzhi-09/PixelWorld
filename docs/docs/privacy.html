Pixel World Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what Pixel World stores on your device, which services it connects to, and what it sends. The Chinese version below is the same policy in Chinese, and it is also shown inside the launcher.

1. We do not collect your information

  • Pixel World has no account system run by the author, collects no usage statistics, uploads no crash reports and uploads none of your files.
  • The author's website (pixelworld.zhuzhi.site) only serves static files for update checks, launcher announcements and documentation. No account information is sent when it is accessed; the website and its host may keep standard access logs such as IP addresses.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your computer or phone:

  • Accounts and sign-in credentials (Microsoft account tokens, third-party authentication tokens, offline account names)
  • AI service endpoints, models and keys, and your chat history with the AI
  • Launcher settings, background images, downloaded game files, worlds and logs

Deleting the launcher's data folder removes this information. On Android, uninstalling the launcher removes its internal data; game files you chose to keep in shared phone storage stay there until you delete them.

3. Services the launcher connects to

When you use the related feature, the launcher connects directly to these services:

ServicePurposeWhat is sent
Microsoft, Xbox Live, MojangMicrosoft account sign-in, game ownership check, skinsAuthorization data and tokens during sign-in
Third-party authentication servers (e.g. LittleSkin)When you choose third-party sign-inThe account and password you enter
Mojang, BMCLAPI and other download mirrors; Forge, NeoForge, Fabric, Quilt and other loader repositoriesDownloading the game, mod loaders and librariesNames of the files to download
ModrinthSearching and downloading mods, modpacks, resource packs and shadersSearch keywords and game version
GitHub and its mirrors, pixelworld.zhuzhi.siteUpdate checks, announcements and documentationCurrent launcher version
Chinese Minecraft WikiMinecraft version news on the home pageNo personal information
Minecraft servers you queryServer status and player countServer address
The AI provider you configure, and web search services you enableAI assistantWhat you send, plus logs and files you allow the AI to read

LAN transfer goes directly between devices on the same local network and does not pass through any server.

These services are run by third parties; how they handle data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Microsoft sign-in uses Microsoft's official OAuth device code flow. You sign in on Microsoft's own page, so the launcher never sees your Microsoft password. The resulting tokens are kept only on your device.

4. Android permissions

  • Network: downloading the game, signing in, checking for updates, etc.
  • All files access: requested only if you choose to keep game files in shared phone storage, to read and write that folder.
  • Install apps: used only when you confirm a launcher update, to install the new version.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. After an update, the launcher asks you to confirm it again at startup.

6. Contact

For questions, please open an issue on GitHub: github.com/zhuzhi-09/PixelWorld/issues

Pixel World 隐私说明

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

本说明介绍 Pixel World 会在你的设备上保存什么、会连接哪些服务，以及发送什么内容。

1. 我们不收集你的信息

  • 本软件没有作者运营的账户系统，不收集使用统计，不上传崩溃报告，也不上传你的文件。
  • 作者的网站（pixelworld.zhuzhi.site）只提供检查更新、启动器公告和使用文档所需的静态文件。访问时不会发送你的账户信息；网站和托管服务可能按惯例记录 IP 地址等访问日志。

2. 保存在你设备上的信息

以下内容只保存在你的电脑或手机上：

  • 账户信息和登录凭据（Microsoft 账户的登录令牌、外置登录的令牌、离线账户名）
  • AI 服务的接口地址、模型和密钥，以及你和 AI 的聊天记录
  • 启动器的设置、背景图片、下载的游戏文件、存档和日志

删除启动器的数据文件夹即可清除这些信息。安卓上卸载启动器会清除应用内部的数据；如果你选择把游戏文件放在手机存储里，这部分文件会保留，需要你自己删除。

3. 会连接的服务

使用相应功能时，启动器会直接连接以下服务：

服务用途发送的内容
Microsoft、Xbox Live、Mojang正版登录、检查游戏所有权、获取皮肤登录过程中的授权信息和令牌
外置登录服务（例如 LittleSkin）你选择外置登录时你填写的账户和密码
Mojang、BMCLAPI 等下载镜像，Forge、NeoForge、Fabric、Quilt 等加载器仓库下载游戏、加载器和运行库要下载的文件名称
Modrinth搜索和下载模组、整合包、资源包、光影搜索关键词和游戏版本
GitHub 及其加速镜像、pixelworld.zhuzhi.site检查更新、读取公告和使用文档当前版本号
中文 Minecraft Wiki首页的 Minecraft 版本动态无个人信息
你查询的 Minecraft 服务器服务器查询、服务器在线人数服务器地址
你配置的 AI 服务商，以及你开启的联网搜索服务AI 助手你发送的内容，以及你允许 AI 读取的日志和文件

局域网互传只在同一局域网内的设备之间直接传输，不经过任何服务器。

这些服务由第三方运营，它们如何处理数据，以各自的隐私政策为准。

4. 安卓权限

  • 网络：下载游戏、登录、检查更新等。
  • 所有文件访问权限：只在你选择把游戏文件放在手机存储里时请求，用来读写这个文件夹。
  • 安装应用：只在你确认更新启动器时使用，用来安装新版本。

5. 说明的更新

本说明可能会更新。更新后，启动器会在启动时请你再次确认。

6. 联系方式

有问题，请在 GitHub 上提交 Issue：https://github.com/zhuzhi-09/PixelWorld/issues
